— TRAP TWO: THE LOYALTY THAT ISN'T LOYALTY —

[55] In 1990, Richard Thaler, Daniel Kahneman, and Jack Knetsch ran a simple experiment at Cornell University.

[74] Here's the trap that doesn't have a villain-movie name. It has a boring one. That's exactly why it works.
[75] In 1985, Hersh Shefrin and Meir Statman published a study most investors have never heard of.

[77] It has one job: it makes you sell your winners too early and hold your losers too long.

[82] Shefrin and Statman found this in almost every retail portfolio they studied.
[83] Winners sold, on average, weeks before losers.

[85] Because selling a winner feels like a victory you get to keep.
[86] And selling a loser feels like making the loss real.
