NEUROCENTS — CREATOR BRIEF FOR VIDIQ
Script Style, Outlier Rules & Retention Framework
=====================================================


CHANNEL
-------
Neurocents. Behavioral finance and money psychology. Faceless, 2D animated, English.
One bias per video. Audience: people who have tried budgeting and failed, earn reasonable
money but save little, recognize themselves as intelligent but financially stuck.


CHARACTER
---------
Alex: large beige oval head, transparent glass skull revealing a pink cartoon Brain Villain
inside. The Villain is Alex's bias personified — not an external enemy, a built-in program.
Every video shows the Villain running, being named, and either inoculated against or
structurally bypassed. Alex never defeats the Villain through willpower. He builds systems
around it.


TITLE FORMULAS — VALIDATED BY OUTLIER DATA
-------------------------------------------
Use these exact structures. Ranked by breakout multiplier:

[Number] Ways Your Brain [Physical Verb] to Stay Broke (Without Telling You)
  Example: "5 Ways Your Brain Physically Rewires Itself to Stay Broke"
  Multiplier: 181x

Why [X] Fails Every [Universal Trigger]
  Example: "Why Willpower Fails Every Payday"
  Multiplier: 132.7x

[Number] [Things] That [Negative Consequence to Brain/Money]
  Example: "3 Traps That Rewire Your Brain to Stay Broke"
  Multiplier: 104x

The [Mechanism] That [Financial Consequence]
  Example: "The Billion-Dollar Bet Your Bank Wins Every Day"
  Multiplier: 36x

Rules:
- Negative framing always beats positive. "Fails" beats "How To". "Broke" beats "Richer".
- Specificity beats generality. "Every Payday" beats "When You Get Paid".
- The title must match the core mechanism of the video — not a surface description of it.
- Max 12 words. Every word must earn its place.


HOOK — CANONICAL STRUCTURE (10 beats, ~30 seconds)
----------------------------------------------------
Beat 1:  IDENTITY — viewer recognizes themselves before they can click away
         Example: "You've started a budget at least three times. It never survived payday."
Beat 2:  "Not because [wrong cause A]."
Beat 3:  "Not because [wrong cause B]."
Beat 4:  "Because [the real mechanism]." — first reveal, reframes the problem
Beat 5:  Promise — one-line solution tease (specific, concrete)
Beat 6:  DATA ANCHOR — exact figure with context (3.5% to 13.6%, 8 min vs 19 min)
Beat 7:  "No [X]. No [Y]. No [Z]." — eliminate the obvious objections
Beat 8:  Specificity of time/place — contrast between two states
Beat 9:  Brain Villain active — calculating, anticipating, already working
Beat 10: Open loop — this video was built to dismantle it

Critical rule: Beat 1 is always identity, never mechanism. The viewer must see themselves
in Beat 1 before they understand what the video is about.


SCRIPT VOICE — RULES
---------------------
- Short sentences. Never more than 20 words per beat.
- Alternate long beats with very short ones. "Alex does." is a complete beat.
- Use "Same X. Same Y. Same result." for repetition patterns — high retention device.
- Use "Not X. Not Y. Not Z." to eliminate false causes before the real one.
- No "basically", no hedging, no filler. Every word is load-bearing.
- Second person ("you", "your brain") only at high-stakes moments: hook, CTA, villain
  inoculation. Overuse kills it.
- Experiments are narrated cinematically: Setup > Conflict > Number > Interpretation.
  Never as lecture.
- Specific amounts: 400 euros, 800 euros — not "some money".


EXPERIMENTS — FORMAT FOR HIGH RETENTION
-----------------------------------------
"[Name] — [Institution] — [year if relevant] —"
"[What they measured, one sentence.]"
"[Setup, 2-3 short beats.]"
"[The result in exact numbers.]"
"[One-line interpretation.]"

Available experiments (already written, reference for continuity):
- Baumeister (1998): radishes vs. cookies > 8 minutes vs. 19 minutes > ego depletion
- Thaler & Benartzi (2004): Save More Tomorrow > 3.5% to 13.6% in five years
- Danziger et al.: judges > 65% to 11% parole grants > decision fatigue
- Schultz: dopamine fires at prediction, not reward > Anticipation Burn


CTA — POSITION AND FORMULA
---------------------------
- Place CTA at ~35-40% through the video — after maximum pain, before solution is revealed.
- Never at the beginning. Never past 50%.
- Optimal moment: immediately after the viewer understands the depth of the problem.

Fixed formula:
  "If your brain is running these programs right now — subscribe."
  "Every week: one bias. How it works. Who exploits it. And what you can actually do about it."

The CTA must mirror the identity from Beat 1.


BRAIN VILLAIN INOCULATION — USE IN EVERY VIDEO
------------------------------------------------
This section appears near the end, before the identity close. It names the viewer's
resistance to the solution in real time — as they are feeling it.

"The Brain Villain has one response to [mechanism/solution]."
"You're feeling it right now."
"Not [obvious emotion]. Something quieter."
"Something that sounds like [virtue]: [the objection]."
"That thought is not intuition. Not wisdom. Not [virtue]."
"It is [bias name] wearing the costume of [virtue]."

This works because it names the objection before the viewer acts on it.
It is not persuasion — it is identification. High retention, high subscription correlation.


IDENTITY CLOSE — FORMULA
--------------------------
Never moralize. Never say "you can do better". Reframe the programs as rational —
just mismatched to environment. Maximum 9 beats.

"The programs are not broken."
"They are perfectly designed for an environment where [evolutionary context]."
"[Why the behavior made sense in that environment.]"
"The Brain Villain was built for that world. Not this one."
"[The structural solution] is the first system designed for the world [Alex] actually lives in."


IMAGE PROMPTS — VISUAL-FIRST RULE
-----------------------------------
Every image must add information the narration cannot convey.
Never illustrate what is already being said.

Text in images ONLY for:
- Numbers and percentages (3.5%, 8:00, 400 euros)
- Proper names and institutions (ROY BAUMEISTER / Florida State)
- Villain's spoken thoughts (speech bubble: "What if I need that money?")
- Essential visual props where the text IS the prop (CANCELLED stamp on calendar)

Never write in the image:
- Concept labels that the narration just said ("EGO DEPLETION", "FINITE RESOURCE")
- Category words the narration lists ("LUNCH / EMAIL / OUTFIT" — use icons instead)
- Verdict words that repeat the conclusion ("GAVE UP", "NO DECISION REQUIRED")

Replace text labels with: posture (figure slumped = gave up), icons (fork = lunch),
visual metaphor (empty space = gone). If the image needs a text label to be understood,
the image concept is too abstract — redesign the image.

------------------------------------------------------------
2D flat cartoon illustration, thick solid black outlines, clean solid color fills,
no gradients. ALEX: large beige oval head, transparent glass upper skull revealing
pink cartoon brain villain, small black dot eyes, thin neutral mouth, black spiky hair,
BLUE t-shirt, gray pants. Brain villain: pink cartoon brain character, heavy-lidded eyes,

blue shirt, gray pants, green for savings/gains, red for loss/danger,
white for diagram scenes. 16:9, 1280x720.

BLUE t-shirt. Not red. Verify every time.


WHAT WE FIXED IN V12 — AND WHY IT MATTERS FOR FUTURE VIDEOS
-------------------------------------------------------------
These are the specific mistakes corrected in V12. Avoid repeating them.

MISTAKE 1 — CTA too late
  Original: CTA at beat 60 of 107 = 56% through the video.
  Viewer had already decided whether to subscribe before the CTA appeared.
  Fix: CTA moved to beat 40 of 102 = 39%, immediately after the emotional low point.
  Rule going forward: CTA goes after maximum pain, before solution reveal. Never past 45%.

MISTAKE 2 — Images repeating the narration
  Original: 15 image prompts contained text labels that repeated the narration exactly.
  The animation was illustrating the audio instead of adding a second information layer.
  Examples: "EGO DEPLETION" written in image while narration says "ego depletion".
  "GAVE UP" written under stopwatch while narration says "gave up in eight minutes".
  Fix: All 15 replaced with icon-based or posture-based visuals.
  Rule going forward: if you can remove the text from the image and the image still
  communicates the same thing through visuals — remove the text.

MISTAKE 3 — Identity Close too long
  Original: 14 beats including a recap of the three traps already explained.
  Viewer felt the video had ended 5 beats before it actually ended.
  Fix: Trimmed to 9 beats. Removed trap recap, kept evolutionary reframe and payoff line.
  Rule going forward: Identity Close maximum 9 beats. Cut everything after the
  evolutionary reframe has landed.

MISTAKE 4 — Visual stutter in hook
  Original: Beats 2 and 3 used the same close-up angle and same expression.
  Looked like a frozen frame — two identical images back to back.
  Fix: Beat 2 eyes down-left (defeat). Beat 3 eyes up-right (searching). Two distinct frames.
  Rule going forward: Never two consecutive beats with the same camera angle,
  same expression, and same background. At minimum change one variable.

MISTAKE 5 — Three diagram beats with no character
  Original: Beats 24-26 were three consecutive abstract diagrams with no Alex, no villain.
  Dead zone in retention — no human anchor.
  Fix: Beat 25 made Alex-centered — Alex reacts to his own reserve depleting.
  Rule going forward: Maximum two consecutive beats without a character visible.
  Break diagram sequences with a reaction beat.


PRODUCTION DOCUMENT FORMAT
---------------------------
Each video produces 6 files:
1. SCRIPT.docx / SCRIPT.pdf — narration with numbered beats and section headers
2. ELEVENLABS.pdf — clean narration only, paste directly into TTS
3. PRODUCTION.pdf — every beat with: Image Prompt, Camera, Lighting, Mood,
   Character Action, Video Motion
4. IMAGE_PROMPTS.pdf — 4 parts of ~26 beats each, full STYLE preamble prepended
   to each prompt, ready for Google Flow / Imagen 4
5. Prepublish_Checklist.docx — title, description, tags, Reddit posts, YouTube Studio config

Script naming convention: build_v[N]_final_script.py, build_v[N]_final_doc.py,
build_v[N]_final_image_prompts_pdf.py, build_v[N]_final_publish_doc.py


SECTION ORDER — EVERY VIDEO
-----------------------------
1. HOOK (10 beats)
2. [WHY THE COMMON SOLUTION FAILS] (8-12 beats) — Alex tried it, it didn't work
3. [THE MECHANISM] (16-20 beats) — scientist + experiment + interpretation
4. CTA (2 beats) — at ~35-40% mark
5. [THE MECHANISM CONCLUSION] (2-4 beats) — pivot beat with curiosity gap
6. [THE DECISION / SOLUTION] (14-18 beats) — structural fix, not willpower fix
7. [THE SCIENCE] (10-14 beats) — data proof of solution
8. BRAIN VILLAIN INOCULATION (10-14 beats) — real-time second person
9. IDENTITY CLOSE (9 beats maximum)
10. NEXT VIDEO TEASE (5-6 beats)
