— TRAP ONE: THE MAYBE HIT —
[18] Six months ago, Alex got the same kind of notification. He didn't buy anything that day either.

[20] Wolfram Schultz — University of Cambridge — ran an experiment with monkeys, a light, and a squirt of juice.
[21] Every time the light came on, juice followed two seconds later.
[22] Schultz measured the monkeys' dopamine neurons the entire time.
[23] At first, the neurons fired when the juice arrived.
[24] After a few repetitions, something changed.
[25] The neurons stopped firing for the juice.
[26] They fired for the light.
[27] Not a metaphor. A measured shift in exactly which moment produces the chemical.
